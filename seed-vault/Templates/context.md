# Context handoff title

## Goal

State the requested outcome.

## Current state

Record completed work and its evidence. Label proposals and inferences separately.

## Constraints

State environment, permissions, user preferences, and relevant versions.

## Decisions and rationale

Explain choices supported by evidence and link durable decision records.

## Failed approaches

Record observed attempts and what they ruled out, or state none documented.

## Unknowns and blockers

Make missing facts explicit. Do not fill gaps with guesses.

## Next actions

Describe the next bounded steps and what verification each needs.

## Sources

Link accessible task artifacts and related Keystone IDs with current revisions.
